Cancellation & Refund Policy

A straightforward guide to changing or cancelling your reservation.

Our standard policy

These terms apply to standard room reservations unless a rate, package, or event agreement says otherwise.

Cancel 7 days before arrival

Reservations cancelled at least 168 hours before check-in are generally eligible for a full refund of eligible prepaid amounts.

Inside 7 days

Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged according to the rate selected at booking. Non-refundable rates remain non-refundable.

Changes to your stay

Contact reservations as early as possible to request date, room, or guest changes. Changes depend on availability and price differences.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and bank.

Events and groups

Meetings, weddings, and group reservations may have separate deposits and cancellation terms stated in the event agreement.

Need help?

Send your booking reference and the email used for the reservation so our team can review your request quickly.
